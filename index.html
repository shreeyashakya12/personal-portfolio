My Projects

Clo.She - Clothing Marketplace

Clo.She is a clothing marketplace project designed to allow users to buy and sell clothes through a convenient digital platform.

The project includes features such as user authentication, product browsing, categories, search, filters, product details, shopping cart, checkout, selling clothes, creating listings, seller profiles, and order management.

The project also focuses on clothing reuse and sustainability by encouraging users to sell clothes that they no longer need.

Main Features

  • User Registration and Login
  • Product Search
  • Product Categories
  • Product Filtering
  • Product Details
  • Shopping Cart
  • Checkout
  • Seller Profile
  • Create Clothing Listing
  • Upload Product Photos
  • Manage Listings
